SIMRAS MAPILLARY MANUAL COVERAGE SCOUT
=====================================

Asset: AP_TEMPLE_KANAKA_DURGA
Location: 16.5154, 80.6059

1. Mapillary opens at the asset location.
2. Zoom in and click a GREEN sequence/dot nearest the temple.
3. Use the playback arrows at the top of the image viewer.
4. Confirm that the same temple/gopuram stays visible while the camera MOVES.
5. Prefer a long continuous sequence, ideally 30+ images.
6. Open the three-dot menu -> Advanced / Advanced Options.
7. Copy:
   - Image key (pKey)
   - Sequence key
   - Current Mapillary URL
8. Return to this PowerShell window and paste them when requested.

Do not use isolated photos as reconstruction evidence.
Do not claim dimensions or survey-grade accuracy from Mapillary imagery alone.

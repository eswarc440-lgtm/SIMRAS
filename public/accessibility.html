Accessibility

Accessibility objectives and considerations for public infrastructure information and technical visualizations.

Accessibility objective

SIMRAS should remain usable with keyboard navigation, readable text contrast, meaningful labels, responsive layouts, visible focus states, and text alternatives for informative images.

Known considerations

Interactive GIS maps, complex digital-twin scenes, engineering visualizations, and dense charts may require additional accessible summaries. Where a visualization conveys a critical result, the same result should also be available as readable text or a table.

Browser support

The web application is intended for current desktop and mobile browsers that support modern JavaScript, WebGL where digital twins are used, and standard accessibility APIs.

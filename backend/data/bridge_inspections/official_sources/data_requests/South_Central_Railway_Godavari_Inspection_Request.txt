Subject: Request for bridge inspection / condition records for academic digital-twin research

Requested asset:
Godavari Arch Bridge / Kovvur-Rajahmundry Railway Bridge
Andhra Pradesh
SIMRAS canonical code: AP_BR_00001

Requested records, preferably in machine-readable form:

1. Bridge number / official railway bridge identifier
2. Inspection dates
3. Inspection type
4. Bridge Inspection Register observations
5. Numerical Rating System (NRS), where applicable
6. Condition Rating Number / Overall Rating Number, where applicable
7. Foundation condition
8. Pier / substructure condition
9. Bearing condition
10. PSC / superstructure condition
11. Crack, spalling, corrosion or leakage observations
12. Scour / waterway observations
13. Repair or maintenance recommendations
14. Date of repair / rehabilitation
15. Inspector / engineering authority
16. Photographs or inspection references, where releasable

Purpose:
Academic research for an infrastructure digital twin and condition-prediction
prototype. No synthetic inspection labels will be substituted for unavailable records.

Preferred formats:
CSV, XLSX, database export, PDF Bridge Inspection Register, or scanned inspection report.

Important:
Inventory data alone is useful as engineering evidence but will not be treated as
a structural-condition ML label unless the inspection/condition assessment is explicit.
